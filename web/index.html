Ignisensing Analytics

A burning activity calendar from MODIS + VIIRS fire hotspots

What this will do

  • Combine 0+ years of MODIS and VIIRS hotspots from NASA FIRMS into one consistent record
  • Let you pick an area on a map and a date range
  • This is a great project!
  • a month × year calendar of fire activity, and how this month compares to the historical median
  • Download the results as CSV
